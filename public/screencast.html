Chatnelo – screencast for Shopify app review

  1. Chatnelo admin page (English): the 3-day free trial starts automatically, and the "Choose your plan" section shows 4 plans (Start 70, Basic 150, Growth 400, Pro 1000) billed through the Shopify Billing API.
  2. "Enable and customize the chat" opens the theme editor with the Chatnelo Chat app embed turned on; the chat launcher is visible in the preview. Save.
  3. Online Store product page: the chat launcher is visible (above the Shopify preview bar). The customer asks "Is this snowboard in stock and how much is shipping?" and gets an answer from live store data with a clickable product card.
  4. Cart page: the chat launcher and conversation are available.
  5. Choosing the Basic 150 plan opens Shopify's native "Approve subscription" page: 3-day free trial, first-month discount, test charge on the development store.
