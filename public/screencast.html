Chatnelo – screencast for Shopify app review

  1. Chatnelo admin page: plan selection (Start 70, Basic 150, Growth 400, Pro 1000, Business 5000) billed through Shopify Billing API.
  2. Theme editor → App embeds → Chatnelo Chat enabled (deep link from the app) and saved.
  3. Online Store product page: the chat launcher is visible (also above the Shopify preview bar); the customer asks about the product and shipping and gets an answer with a clickable product card.
  4. Cart page: the chat launcher and conversation are available.
  5. Clicking Select on a plan opens Shopify's native “Approve subscription” page (test charge on the development store).
