Chatnelo — how it works

Install the app, pick a plan (3-day free trial), turn on the chat in the theme editor — your customers get instant answers from your real products, stock and shipping.

  1. Open Chatnelo in Shopify admin and pick a plan.
  2. Approve the subscription with Shopify Billing (3 days free).
  3. Turn on the Chatnelo Chat app embed in the theme editor.
  4. The chat bubble appears on your storefront.
  5. Customers get instant answers about stock, prices and shipping, with clickable product cards.
